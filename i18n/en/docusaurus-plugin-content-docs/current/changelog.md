## In development (not released)

No version number has been set yet.

- **Rig Controller overhaul**: Visual adjustments, direct dragging of IK, pole, and Root handles, Shift to constrain the direction, shoulder FK, per-limb IK/FK switching, and mirroring.
- **Display**: View Preset (As in scene, Neutral, Flat) and a two-color outline.
- **Installing on an avatar**: Adds one Rig Controller under the root.
- **Assist (analysis)**: Load markers with numbers, estimated support, ground offset, and the list of highest-load joints.
