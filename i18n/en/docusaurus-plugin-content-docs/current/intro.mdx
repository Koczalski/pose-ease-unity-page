A Unity editor extension for posing Humanoid avatars for VRChat. You pose the avatar by dragging the Rig Controller in the Scene view, and then check, as numbers, how much load the pose puts on the body.

</Hero>

## Requirements

- Unity 2022.3.22f1 (Built-in Render Pipeline)
- A Humanoid avatar (with an Animator and an Avatar)
- Other Unity versions and render pipelines have not been tested.
