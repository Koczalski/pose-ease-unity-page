## Musculoskeletal models and data used by the analysis
Muscle paths, moment arms, and muscle strengths are taken from the public models below (values are used as published).

| Data | Source | License | Copyright |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leg muscles (Rajagopal 2015) | SimTK "Full Body Model for use in Dynamic Simulations of Human Gait" https://simtk.org/projects/full_body | MIT Use Agreement | Copyright (c) 2015, Stanford University |
| Arm muscles (Arm26) | OpenSim Models, Models/Arm26/arm26.osim https://github.com/opensim-org/opensim-models | CC BY 3.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ (modified: paths tabulated on a joint-angle grid, left arm mirrored, wrapped muscles computed with OpenSim 4.6) | The OpenSim Development Team |
| Lumbar muscles (Christophy et al.) | SimTK "Musculoskeletal Model of the Lumbar Spine" https://simtk.org/projects/lumbarspine | MIT Use Agreement | Copyright (c) 2010, University of California at Berkeley |
| Neck muscles (Vasavada Neck Model 1.6) | SimTK https://simtk.org/frs/?group_id=253 | MIT Use Agreement | Copyright (c) 2016, Stanford University |

In the development repository, the full MIT Use Agreement texts are kept in LICENSE files next to each data file, and the Arm26 source and license are recorded inside its data file. They will be bundled once the distribution format is decided.

## Posture references and load bands
- Posture references: based on public material citing OWAS (Ovako Working posture Analysing System) and ISO 11226:2000.
- Load bands: about 15% can be held for a long time, about 50% for about 2 minutes (Ergoweb). Other boundaries are assumed values.
